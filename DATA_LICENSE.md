`src/student/sets/`, `src/distill/plans/`), the validation outputs (`src/validation/`), the per-decision results

Please cite the paper when you use them.

## AI-generated content

The following files contain content generated by large language models:

* `training_data.tar` (Release asset): teacher trajectories generated by DeepSeek-V4-Pro and DeepSeek-V4-Flash through the
  DeepSeek API, and self-training trajectories sampled from the authors' fine-tuned Qwen3-1.7B student. Each record carries
  `meta.ai_content` = `{ai_generated, generator, generated_utc}` (for DPO pairs, one entry for each of `chosen` and
  `rejected`).
* `results/api/`: decisions and token usage of the API models (DeepSeek, Kimi, Qwen, GPT) in the round-1 experiments; each
  row records the requested model (`model`), the served model (`served_model`, where returned) and the call time (`t_utc`,
  where recorded).
* `results/student/`: complete trajectories of the authors' student models.

The DeepSeek terms of service permit using model outputs, including for training other models. Users of the
DeepSeek-generated trajectories must also comply with those terms and keep the AI-generated labels when redistributing the
data. Tool results and feedback messages inside the trajectories are produced by the hydraulic simulator in `src/`.
